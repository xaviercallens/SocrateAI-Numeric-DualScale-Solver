\begin{abstract}
We present the benchmark execution and formal specification roadmap for LeanFlow, 
a dual-scale pseudo-spectral PDE solver designed for end-to-end mathematical 
rigor from continuum formulation through numerical implementation to Lean~4 
kernel verification. We report reproducible results on 10 canonical use cases 
(UC7--UC16) spanning incompressible Navier-Stokes, Boussinesq convection, 
Burgers shock dynamics, and magnetohydrodynamics. Each use case is grounded in 
published reference solutions (PDEBench, Ghia et al., Dedalus, Athena++, JHTDB) 
and accompanied by axiomatic Lean~4 formal specification roadmaps encoding the 
continuum PDE semantics. All numerical results are computed externally in Python 
with SHA-256 certification seals. The formal specifications use \texttt{sorry} 
stubs per the established epistemic protocol and are designated as Formal 
Specification Roadmaps, not verified proofs.
\end{abstract}
