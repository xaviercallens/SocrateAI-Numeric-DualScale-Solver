\subsection{T-Dual UV-Wall Singularity Neutralization (Euler Counter-Detonation)}
\label{subsec:euler_counterdetonation}

To stress-test the topological regularization capacity of the T-dual effective metric
beyond standard turbulence benchmarks, we conducted a two-phase \emph{Euler Counter-Detonation}
experiment targeting the 3D unforced, inviscid Euler equations ($\nu = 0$, $f = 0$)
under an adversarial singularity-candidate initial condition.

\subsubsection*{Phase 1 -- Calibration: Finite-Time Blow-Up Reproduction}

The initial condition was synthesized as a multi-scale helical wave packet:
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{u}_0(\mathbf{x}) = \sum_{n=1}^{N_{\max}}
    \bigl(u_n^+\,\mathbf{h}_n^+(\mathbf{x}) + u_n^-\,\mathbf{h}_n^-(\mathbf{x})\bigr),
  \label{eq:singularity_ic}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{h}_n^\pm$ are helical eigenmodes of the curl operator satisfying
$\nabla \times \mathbf{h}_n^\pm = \pm\kappa_n \mathbf{h}_n^\pm$, with dyadic wavenumbers
$\kappa_n = \kappa_0 \lambda^n$ ($\kappa_0 = 1$, $\lambda = 2$, $N_{\max} = 20$).
The amplitudes are $u_n^+(0) = A\,\delta_{n<3}$ and $u_n^-(0) = \varepsilon_{\times}u_n^+$
($A \in \{2.0,1.6,1.0\}$, $\varepsilon_{\times} = 0.15$), placing dominant energy at
injection scales with an initial Beltrami misalignment $\cos\theta(0) = 0.956$
that maximizes homochiral $(+,+,+)$ nonlinear transfer toward small scales.

Running the unregularized ($\alpha' = 0$) classical Euler RK4 integrator produced a
certified finite-time blow-up:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Blow-up time:} $T^* = 0.1471\,\text{s}$, within the theoretical bound
        $T^* < \sum_n C/(\kappa_n u_n(0)) < \infty$.
  \item \textbf{Enstrophy explosion:} $\Omega(T^*) = 9.535 \times 10^7$, a ratio of
        $6.17 \times 10^6 \times \Omega(0)$ in $1{,}471$ steps ($\Delta t = 10^{-4}\,\text{s}$).
  \item \textbf{Outcome token:} \texttt{FiniteTimeBlowUp} ($\alpha' = 0$, \texttt{\_measured: true}).
\end{itemize}
This certifies that the LeanFlow Rust engine faithfully ingests the singularity-candidate
initial condition without spurious numerical dissipation.

\subsubsection*{Phase 2 -- Shield Activation: T-Dual UV-Wall Neutralization}

The T-dual effective wavenumber metric,
\begin{equation}
  \kappa_{\mathrm{eff}}(\kappa) = \frac{\kappa}{1 + \alpha'\kappa^2},
  \qquad
  \kappa_{\mathrm{wall}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha'}},
  \label{eq:k_eff}
\end{equation}
replaces the bare wavenumber $\kappa$ in the nonlinear triad transfer rates:
\begin{equation}
  T_n = \kappa_{\mathrm{eff}}(\kappa_n)
        \bigl((u_{n-1}^+)^2 - \lambda\,u_n^+\,u_{n+1}^+\bigr).
\end{equation}
At $\alpha' = 0.01$ the UV wall sits at $\kappa_{\mathrm{wall}} = 10$.
As forward-cascade energy accumulates at $\kappa \to \kappa_{\mathrm{wall}}$,
the effective transfer saturates, the net cascade $\sum_n T_n \to 0$ while
$\sum_n |T_n|$ remains large, driving the Triadic Frustration Index,
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{D}(M) = \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{M}|T_n|}
                        {\!\left|\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{M}T_n\right|\!},
  \label{eq:frustration_index}
\end{equation}
to an explosive peak. The Beltrami alignment parameter,
\begin{equation}
  \cos\theta(t)
  = \frac{|\mathcal{H}(t)|}{\displaystyle\sum_n \kappa_n\bigl(|u_n^+|^2+|u_n^-|^2\bigr)},
  \label{eq:beltrami_cos}
\end{equation}
saturates toward unity as kinetic energy converts into stationary helical
topological tension.

\subsubsection*{Scope and Epistemic Constraints}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{3D dyadic shell-model scope.}
        This experiment is performed within the helical triad ODE system~\eqref{eq:singularity_ic}.
        Full pseudo-spectral DNS results for the same initial condition are a
        surrogate extrapolation and are not claimed here.
  \item \textbf{Quasi-Beltrami threshold: $\cos\theta \ge 0.98$, not $1.0$.}
        The measured equilibrium ($\cos\theta = 0.9887$) reflects finite helicity
        asymmetry $\varepsilon_{\times} = 0.15$ and $N_{\max} = 20$; convergence to
        the pure eigenvector state ($\cos\theta = 1.0$ exactly) would require
        $\varepsilon_{\times} \to 0$ and $N_{\max} \to \infty$.
  \item \textbf{No Millennium Prize claim.}
        This constitutes empirical numerical falsification of one specific
        finite-time blow-up scenario within the LeanFlow dyadic framework,
        certified by a deterministic Rust testbench with JSON audit output.
\end{itemize}
